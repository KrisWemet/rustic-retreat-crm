# Financial record and reminders

Each wedding has an optional **signed contract total**, entered by an admin from the actual agreement. The immutable package snapshot and reception overage remain visible for comparison. Until the signed total is entered, the CRM shows the total and outstanding balance as **unknown**. It never infers GST or an unapproved fee.

Optional charges start **pending**. Only an approved charge marked **outside the signed total** adds to total due. Mark a charge included in the contract when it is already in the signed amount; waived and pending charges do not add to the balance. The balance is signed total plus approved extras outside it, less all recorded receipts. Overpayment is shown as a credit to reconcile. The installment schedule is separate and a mismatch with total due is flagged.

Record each payment receipt separately, including manually received e-transfers, with the actual date, method, and optional bank reference. It can be linked to a scheduled installment or left unallocated. Linked receipts update the installment's paid amount; the database rejects a different-booking link or an amount above that installment. Admins can amend installment labels, amounts, and due dates without changing the receipt-derived paid amount. An unpaid installment can be removed; linked receipts must be removed or reallocated first. Earlier cumulative paid amounts migrate into one historical receipt marked **Other** and requiring bank reconciliation. The damage deposit remains in its separate checkout record and is not included in the wedding balance.

The **Reminders** page shows unpaid scheduled items and explicitly marked couple planning tasks due within seven days or overdue. A paid item or completed task disappears. The channel comes from the inquiry's current preferred contact method: email, text, or phone. If that preference or address is missing, the CRM asks for correction instead of defaulting to SMS. A contact link opens the local email/text/call composer; **the CRM does not send a message itself**. After actual delivery, an admin records it with notes. The database logs channel, recipient, actor, and time, and rejects an ineligible, completed, wrong-channel, or repeat reminder within seven days. The history is admin-only.

Admins can allocate or reallocate existing receipts using **Apply to installment**. Once receipts settle the overall wedding balance, payment reminders stop even if an installment still needs allocation reconciliation. Planning-task reminders remain independent. Email reminders use the optional reminder email, falling back to the primary inquiry email; this keeps the primary email stable for website submission matching.

Automated sending would require a provider, explicit templates/consent, and delivery callbacks; it is not configured here.
